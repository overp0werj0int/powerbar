# Preferences behavior

Powerbar Preferences is one overlay window on the focused monitor with four pages, Layout, Groups, Look and Behavior: Layout and Groups draw the bar as an exploded view, Look and Behavior are desks of strips on one of its tilted plates. Every control is reachable from the keyboard, a legend or the footer lists the keys that apply to whatever has focus, and every change shows on the bar as it is made. Everything in Preferences can also be done from the command line (integration.md, CLI section).

Sources: Preferences.qml, PreferencesModel.js, TuningModel.js, Tuning.qml, DeskModel.js, ShelfModel.js, SliderModel.js, IconSearch.js, DeskPage.qml, DeskStrip.qml, DeskChoice.qml, DeskRow.qml, Fader.qml, SliderDraft.qml, LookEditor.qml, BehaviorEditor.qml, PrefsChoice.qml, PrefsToggle.qml, LayoutEditor.qml, GroupsEditor.qml, GroupsEditorModel.js, CameraShaft.qml, BeamShade.qml, TuningEditor.qml, LayerPlate.qml, ZoneTile.qml, CenterSideDot.qml, InkIcon.qml, LayerPicker.qml, IconPicker.qml, ShortcutRow.qml, tests/preferences.test.cjs, tests/desk.test.cjs, tests/shelf.test.cjs, tests/slider.test.cjs, tests/icon-search.test.cjs, tests/groups-editor.test.cjs, tests/tuning.test.cjs.

## Window and lifecycle

- **PREFS-1** Preferences open on the focused monitor when the nine-dot Preferences button at the end of the bar is clicked or chosen from the keyboard mode (it opens on that bar's screen, and ends the keyboard mode first), or by `omarchy-shell powerbar preferences`, `preferencesPage <n>` or `groupsPreferences`. While open, the button shows its selected highlight. Opening closes any open drawer and the tooltip, and the keyboard mode cannot be started (`keysOn` answers `error: not while Preferences are open`). There is one window; opening it on another monitor moves it there. Code: Preferences.show(), open()

- **PREFS-2** Preferences close on Esc (from any control that does not use Esc itself: an open dropdown, a lifted widget, the icon picker and a slider with an unapplied value take it first), the Close button (esc) at the top right, or `omarchy-shell powerbar closePreferences` (no effect when closed). The card is min(screen width - 48, 1180) wide and min(screen height - 64, 790) tall, in scaled px, and fades in over 140 ms (scale 0.98 to 1 over 180 ms; no motion with animations off).
- **PREFS-92** `,` closes Preferences too (when the focused control does not take it; no Ctrl, Alt or Super), and returns to the keyboard mode when Preferences were opened from it (keyboard.md KEYS-81). Code: Preferences.qml closeToKeys()

- **PREFS-3** Preferences read the live config: a change from the CLI, another Preferences control or a file edit shows in the controls as it arrives.

- **PREFS-102** Preferences are a page, not the whole screen: a sheet held off the screen by the floating bar's edge gap (`Bar.edgeGap`, 4 px) on its three free sides and, on the bar's side, by what a floating bar would take on that screen whatever the style now (its thickness: round the camera on a notched top edge where a floating bar wraps the notch, `NotchModel.floatingThickness`, else its size; plus its gap off the screen) and the same gap again between that border and the page. So the bar stays in view and changes show on it, the gap to a floating bar's border is the gap to the screen's sides, and the page's top never moves when the style changes. The one bar thicker than the floating one, the Notch style on a screen without a camera (its bar window as tall as `screenNotch.height`), pushes the page down by what it takes more, so the gap to its body is the same gap (`Bar.screenExtents`, each screen's bar window thickness). The sheet is the popups background, opaque, with the floating bar's rounding (`Style.cornerRadius`, square on a square theme) and a 1 px edge in the popups border colour, no shadow and no dimmed area; around it the desktop shows. The window covers the screen but the bar's band and is transparent there: a click on the margin does nothing and never reaches a window under it; keyboard focus (Exclusive), the exclusion mode (Ignore) and the layer (Overlay) are as before. Everything inside is measured from the sheet's edges: the header (title, page tabs, Close) at the top, inset by the page's margin (66 px, less on a narrow screen: a 23rd of the width), the plates, and the camera's cap hanging from the sheet's top edge (PREFS-24). Every page draws its keys as a legend (PREFS-103) and has no footer unless a save failed. Text on every page is antialiased in grey, never with LCD subpixels (Qt Quick's default distance-field text uses them on desktop GPUs whatever the window, and on this translucent window grey text took blue and orange fringes): Powerbar's own text is GrayText (curve rendering, sharp on the tilted plates too), and Omarchy's controls' text still in the default rendering is switched to it as Preferences open, change page or move focus (RenderModel.grayText). Code: Bar.floatingThicknessFor(), Bar.screenExtents, noteExtent(), NotchModel.floatingThickness(); Preferences.qml window.barInset, window.gap, card, card.inset, grayControls, sheet; LayoutEditor measureAxis (pageTop); GrayText.qml.
- **PREFS-103** The Layout page's keys are a legend at the page's bottom left, beside the inspector: one row per key, a column of keycaps and what they do, 10 px apart, the labels in the caption size at 70 %, as large as a strip's caption (PREFS-125) and the keycaps' own text, on every page that draws one. It shows the keys for whatever has focus (the layers, a lifted chip, the inspector's focused control, or "tab The layers" when nothing has focus) and leaves out the pages' digits and esc Close, which the header shows. It sits 8 px above the page's bottom, at most 640 px wide and never under the inspector, in front of the layers; the layers only scroll under it when the inspector is out and the sheet has made room for it. The Look and Behavior pages draw the same legend at the same place, with their own lines (PREFS-130, PREFS-131). Code: LayoutEditor legend, hints; DeskPage legend.
- **PREFS-104** The cursor's chip rises 8 px off its tile and grows with the rise to 103 %, in its tile's own opaque colour under its fill so nothing shows through it, drawn over its neighbours with a shadow 3 px further below it than it has risen: one flat black band in the chip's shape, 30 % on a dark theme and 20 % on a light one, no blur or rings, so it reads as the slabs' edges and the inspector's shadow do and nothing shimmers while it moves; and the tile it is on rises 10 px off its layer, its 6 px dark edge (only the band under the tile is drawn, from its bottom edge down, half a pixel in from each side so none of it shows along the tile's sides under the tilt; square at the top, rounded at the bottom) reaching down to the line it rested on as one block (16 px while it is up, no seam, no second tone), so a lifted tile reads as lifted and not as one drawn a little higher; both glide up in 120 ms and drop in the same frame the cursor leaves (Rise.qml), and the chip's fill, its growth and its shadow go with it in that frame (no colour fade on a chip), so a cursor moving fast leaves no trail of settling chips or tiles; at once both ways with animations off. The chip pulled out to the inspector casts the same band 6 px below it. The Groups page's chips cast it the same way (a list's chip rises 4 px, a pool's 8). Every tilted rectangle (slabs, edges, chips, the camera's cap) is drawn antialiased, and slab edges are 30 % on a light theme instead of 45 %. A chip pulled out to the inspector (its outline stays), a lifted chip and the Hidden layer's and the tray's slabs do not rise. The leader follows the rise. Code: LayoutEditor Tile.raise, Cell.raise.
- **PREFS-105** On a preview with a camera, a dashed leader in the accent colour, one colour all along, runs from the top middle of the bar layer's Center tile over the cap to a dot beside the camera, 16 px out from the cap on the side Center sits on (ZONE-96), with "Center" written outside it. The dot is a Tab stop, and the dot (28 px round it) and its "Center" caption are one pointer target (resting on either lights the dot's ring; a click or a drag starts on either): h/l (Left/Right) put Center left or right of the camera, Space or Enter and a click swap sides, and a drag of more than 6 px carries the dot and lands on the side of the screen's middle it is released on. The change is saved at once (`powerbarCenterSide`, cleared for the default right) and the real bar moves its Center widgets to that side; the dot glides there in 160 ms. The legend shows "h l Center left or right of the camera", "space Swap sides", "tab The layers". Code: LayoutEditor centerSide, setCenterSide, sideDot.

- **PREFS-106** The layer stack has a fixed height: the page below the header's band (36 px) down to the legend's band, which is as tall as the legend's longest list (eight rows) plus 24 px above and 8 px below it, whatever the legend shows, so the legend and the inspector never move and the layers never resize as focus moves. Each layer asks for the height its fullest zone needs (its name, its rows of chips, a lifted chip's place included); while the sum and the gaps fit, every layer gets it. When they do not, each layer starts at its floor (its name and one row; the tray one row) and the rest is shared out: every layer takes what it asks for up to an equal share of what is left, so modest layers leave the rest to the others and layers that all want more get the same (three layers over the room get a third each). When not even the floors fit, the floors win and the legend gives way. A zone with more rows than its layer gives it scrolls inside its tile: the chips show in a window from under the zone's name to 2 px above the tile's bottom, cut at its edges, with the tile's colour fading over the cut chips at an edge there is more beyond (20 px at the top, 14 px at the bottom), and a hairline thumb (1.5 px, rounded) of the text colour (30 %, 50 % on the cursor's tile) in the tile's right pad, as long as the window's share of the rows, only while the zone overflows; no track, no arrows. All of it sits on the tile, so it tilts with the layer and rises with the tile. The wheel over the tile scrolls it a row a notch (or by the touchpad's pixels); the cursor's chip, and a lifted chip's landing place, scroll into view with 10 px to spare above for the raise; a pointer drag held within 24 px of the window's top or bottom edge scrolls it at about 190 px/s while there is more that way, the landing place following; a chip scrolled out of the window is not under the pointer. The inspector, when out, overlaps the lower layers as before (PREFS-101). Code: PreferencesModel.shareLayers(), tiltedPlaneHeight(); LayoutEditor plateDemands, plateFloors, legendBand, stackRoom, Cell.scroll, reveal, scrollBy, revealCursor, autoScroll.
- **PREFS-107** The Hidden layer is one slab, like the tray's (PREFS-97), not five zones: a solid tile across the five zones' columns (its colour and edge, no zone names), its chips flowing five to a row, 8 px apart, in bar order zone by zone (Left's first, then Left of center and on; within a zone in its order), at half strength (PREFS-22). A hidden widget keeps its zone in the config, so it comes back where it was; the inspector's Zone shows and sets it. The camera's shade goes on over the slab where it lies over Center. The slab does not rise with the cursor. Empty: "Nothing hidden. x on a widget, or a drag here, keeps it off the bar.", and the inspector on it: "Nothing hidden yet. Hidden widgets keep their zone, off the bar. Send one here with x, or drag one here." Keys on a slab (the tray's too): h/l walk along its chips, j/k go a row down or up (from a row above a shorter last row, j goes to the last chip), and past the first or last row on to the next layer, at its first chip going down and its last going up; leaving a slab for a layer of zones lands in the zone of the chip the cursor is on (a tray icon still in the tray: the zone the cursor last had, else Right), coming into one from a zone lands on its first or last chip. Shift with h or l on a hidden chip moves it to the next zone, still hidden, at its zone's end, so it only changes place in the slab when it passes another zone's chips. A lifted chip, a drag or Shift+j/k into the slab keeps the widget's zone (a tray icon takes the zone the bar's System tray sits in, else Right) and only orders it among that zone's hidden widgets: it goes before the first chip of its zone from the landing place on, else to its zone's end. The legend then says "h l Along the slab", "j k A row down or up, on to the next layer" and, on a hidden chip, "x Bring it back from the hidden layer". Code: PreferencesModel.slab(), slabPos(), slabBefore(), across(), stepLayer(); LayoutEditor slabbed, zoneOf, homed, cellEntries, rowsOf, Plate.slab, Cell.flows, drop(), step(), carryLayer.
- **PREFS-108** One accent focus ring follows keyboard focus across every page: a 1 px hairline 5 px outside the focused control, in the theme's shape (the theme's corner rounding plus 5 px, square when the theme is square), gliding to the next control in 120 ms; lists and grids that draw their own cursor opt out (ownsFocusRing). Code: Preferences focusRing.

- **PREFS-4** On opening, Preferences list the saved widget images in `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/powerbar/widgets` (default `~/.cache/powerbar/widgets`), read the widget glyphs the bar recorded (`glyphs.json` next to it), and take fresh images of the widgets on the target screen. Hidden or unseen widgets are drawn from the saved image, else their own glyph, else Powerbar's stock glyph for that widget.

- **PREFS-5** A write that cannot be queued or that the host refuses shows "Could not save. Check that shell.json is writable." in the footer (red, right side); the bar returns to the saved config. A layout move names the widget and the neighbour it goes before by instance key (`PreferencesModel.anchorKey`), so it still lands beside that neighbour after another change to the layout; one whose widget or neighbour is gone shows "The layout changed elsewhere. Try again." and one that cannot be queued "Could not save the layout. Check that shell.json is writable.". Shortcut failures: KEYS-53.

## Pages and tabs

- **PREFS-6** Four pages, in a tab row at the top with their number as a keycap: Layout (1), Groups (2), Look (3), Behavior (4). With `powerbarTuning` on (INTEG-155) there is a fifth, Tuning (5), its tab at the header's right just left of Close (PREFS-132).

- **PREFS-7** A page is chosen by clicking its tab, pressing 1 to 4 (5 while the Tuning page is there; when no control uses the key), Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+4 (Ctrl+5 likewise; also while typing in a field), Ctrl+Tab (next, wrapping, the Tuning page included) or Ctrl+Shift+Tab (previous). The active tab is bold with an accent underline.

- **PREFS-8** A page opening takes focus to its first control: Layout the layer the cursor is on, Groups its first plate (the cursor on the chosen group's face, PREFS-117), Look and Behavior the control the cursor was last on there, else the desk's first (PREFS-126).

- **PREFS-12** Superseded by PREFS-125 (every strip of a desk takes the tallest one's height).

- **PREFS-16** Tab, Shift+Tab, Backtab and Shift+Backtab all jump (Qt reports Shift+Tab as ambiguous on some layouts; the direction comes from the key itself).

## Strip structure

- **PREFS-17** A strip has its title, an optional caption (a short muted line), its controls top to bottom, an optional footnote and its faders at the bottom, one under the other (PREFS-125).

- **PREFS-18** Strips have no Reset action: pressing `r` does nothing and no Reset button is shown. Shape, Surface and Shadow go back to the theme's values by choosing Theme in their Scope chips (your values are kept for later); a single fader goes back to the theme's or its default value with Backspace, the Notch faders to the camera's size, singly or both at once with the Notch strip's action row (PREFS-95).

- **PREFS-19** Superseded by PREFS-39 and PREFS-117 (Dissolve is on the face and in the inspector).

- **PREFS-20** A control that does not apply is dimmed to 45 % but stays where it is (Border thickness while Borders is off, Softness and Strength while Shadow is off, Background blur and opacity and Shape's controls under the Theme scope), and the cursor's walk passes over it, so the desk does not move as focus does. On the Layout page's inspector such a control carries a one-line reason (PREFS-26).

## Layout page

- **PREFS-21** The Layout page draws the bar as layers, top to bottom: "On the bar" (caption "Always visible", or the zone-fit notes when a zone gave way), "On demand" (caption: "Behind the zone's dots; shown on activity, or when you hover the dots" in inline mode, "In a menu from the zone's dots; shown briefly on activity" in menu mode, "In the zone's drawer when you hover it, or briefly on activity" in drawer mode), "Hidden" ("Kept off the bar"), then the tray's (PREFS-97). Each layer is a plate (PREFS-99) whose first column names it: the title and, 8 px below in the body-small size, the caption; its last column, on the right, says how many it holds ("7 widgets", "1 widget" or "Empty") on the caption's line, in its size, at 40 %. They correspond to `powerbarVisibility` `shown`, `managed`, `hidden`; a widget with no saved value is On the bar. Groups appear on the layer their visibility names. Code: LayoutEditor shelfInfo, Plate.

- **PREFS-22** A plate spans the page (at most 1380 px), centred on the screen's middle, and has seven columns 8 px apart: the layer's name, the five zones in bar order (Left, Left of center, Center, Right of center, Right; on a side bar Top, Above center, Center, Below center, Bottom), and a spare column that mirrors the name's, so Center sits on the screen's middle. The columns share the width equally, except that on a preview of a screen with a camera Center is as wide as the camera (at most one and a half shares) so it sits under it. The zones are tiles: the zone's name, then the widgets as chips one under another in bar order, each one tile wide; an empty tile says "Empty". The Hidden layer is one slab instead (PREFS-107). A tile is 33 px plus 27 per row (chips 24 px tall, 3 apart, 8 px padding, a 20 px name row), and every tile on a layer is as tall as the layer's tallest. A chip shows the widget's icon in a 20 px square (the middle of its ink, or a wide face's first glyph), its name elided to fit, and a group's member count in a pill; chips on the Hidden layer are drawn at half strength. The icon comes from, in order, the widget's live image, a saved image in the current text colour, a group's face, the glyph the widget draws itself, a saved image recoloured, Powerbar's stock glyph. Code: PreferencesModel.layerColumns(); LayoutEditor columns, Cell, Tile; InkIcon.qml.

- **PREFS-23** A side bar is drawn the same way, its zones under their side names, and widgets are drawn horizontally. On a narrow page the columns narrow with it; a chip is never under 80 px.

- **PREFS-24** Center always sits under the screen's middle. On a preview of a screen with a camera at the top, the camera is the notch's cap: solid black from just under the card's outline (the page's top edge plus the card's 1 px border, so the outline runs whole over the camera) down to a torn paper edge a little above the plates, with the camera's eye (a ring round a dot, 16 px) 14 px under the page's top and "camera" 6 px below it, both white at half strength since the cap is black on every theme. It is one shade (shaders/beam.frag, a fragment shader) drawn on the bar layer's own tilted plane, as wide as Center, so its sides run on the lines that layer's Center tile edges lie on and spread with them as they come near (the same quad and sides as before the tear). The tear's straight line is `gapAbove` page px above the bar layer's top edge; the edge wanders `tearDepth` px either way along it, by fractional Brownian value noise across the column (`grain` thousandths of a bump per px, `octaves` octaves, `seed`), antialiased over about a device pixel. Along the tear the paper's lighter core shows: everything above a second torn line of the same grain and depth (`fringeSeed`) plus `fringeWidth` px, at `fringeStrength`, under the black, so it shows only where that line lies below the cap's (the popups' background lightened toward white on a light theme, rgb(0.54, 0.57, 0.71) on a dark one; width 0 is no fringe). Under the tear a soft black shade falls, like a blurred, dropped copy of the torn cap: `shadeStrength` at the edge, fading with a smoothstep over `shadeBlur` px either side of `shadeDrop` px under the edge, which for the shade is itself blurred across (five binomial taps over the same reach) so it does not streak under each tooth; inside the column, or `shadeSpread` px past each side with a raised-cosine fall-off (that spill is a second quad behind the plates, and also on every tile and strip it reaches, cut to each: the Layout page's zone tiles and Hidden slab (not the tray's dashed row), the Groups page's face and drawer tiles, the Look and Behavior pages' strips; ZoneTile spillClip, DeskStrip spillClip). A pixel of noise hides banding, and the column keeps its soft 1.5 px sides against stepping on the tilt. Defaults, the look that ships: `gapAbove` 35, `tearDepth` 8, `grain` 31, `octaves` 3, `seed` 24, `fringeWidth` 12, `fringeStrength` 13 %, `fringeSeed` 12 (another seed than the edge's, so the fringe is ragged along it; the edge's own seed makes it an even band), `shadeBlur` 4, `shadeDrop` 7, `shadeStrength` 16 %, `shadeSpread` 0. Tuning (a singleton) holds them. While the Tuning page is on (`bar.powerbarTuning`, INTEG-155; PREFS-132) it reads them from `~/.cache/powerbar/tuning.json` ($XDG_CACHE_HOME first; a missing or bad file is the defaults, each value clamped to its range: TuningModel.SECTIONS) and writes it 500 ms after the last change; with the page off the file is left alone and the defaults draw. No IPC. Every Center tile and strip under the camera draws the same shade over its own surface and under its chips at its own seen position, with the same numbers, so the tear and the shade cross a tile's top edge without a step, cap to tile to gap. The bar layer's Center tile's top corners are square (the others keep the theme's rounding). With Pages in perspective off it is the same, flat. Without a camera there is no cap and Center sits in its own place. A caption under the On the bar layer is not drawn any more; the inspector's Zone says "left of the camera" or "right of the camera" (PREFS-26). Code: shaders/beam.frag (compiled with qsb to beam.frag.qsb); Tuning.qml, TuningModel.js; PreferencesModel.tiltedDepth(); CameraShaft.qml, BeamShade.qml; LayoutEditor camera, Cell.shaft, Cell.seenTop; tests/tuning.test.cjs.

- **PREFS-25** One layout serves every screen. When the screens differ (one has a camera, another not), a Preview dropdown at the page's top right (over the bar layer's spare column) chooses the screen, labelled with the screen's name and "(camera)" on a notched one; it changes the picture (the camera's shaft and Center's width, the Zone notes), never the layout. It starts on the screen Preferences opened on. Code: LayoutEditor previewName, previewScreen, screensDiffer.

- **PREFS-26** The inspector (PREFS-101) shows the selected widget as the bar draws it, its name and its plugin id (a group: "N widgets in this group"; a tray icon: "In Omarchy's tray" or "Moved out of Omarchy's tray"), then: Layer, a list of sheets (On the bar, On demand, Hidden, and In the tray for a tray icon) with the widget's own filled, one Tab stop where j/k (and the arrows) or a click move the widget at once, (the legend names "j k Down or up a layer" while it has focus); Zone, a small map of the bar (PrefsZoneMap): five equal cells with the camera over Center when the preview has one (a black tab from the cell's top edge, set under the cell's own border when it has one, so a chosen or focused Center keeps its whole border line), the chosen cell filled and marked with the widget's icon; h/l or the arrows walk it, Space or Enter chooses, a click chooses at once, and its caption names the zone under the cursor, with "left of the camera" or "right of the camera" for the center's zones on a notched preview ("on <screen>" when the screens differ); the widget's shortcut row; "Peeks on activity", enabled only with Autohide and its activity option on and a widget that is not clock-driven, with a hint only when the reason is not the toggle itself ("It changes on a clock, so it never brings the bar out", "Turned off for every widget on the Behavior page"); and Group (a dropdown, "Not grouped" plus every group, whose list opens above it since the inspector sits at the window's bottom, its chevron pointing up; Space, Enter or Down opens it, j/k walk it, Enter chooses, Esc closes it and keeps focus on it; choosing a group puts the widget in its drawer; a group shows its members' faces and an "Edit group" button instead). The dropdown is Powerbar's copy of Omarchy's, which only opens downward (PrefsDropdown.qml). Code: PrefsZoneMap.qml, LayerPicker.qml; LayoutEditor Inspector, zoneChoices, cameraNote.

- **PREFS-27** Keyboard: h/l (Left/Right) move the cursor to the next zone on the same layer, j/k (Down/Up) walk the zone's chips and empty tiles (valid resting places; an empty zone tile under the cursor is told by its name reading bold, an empty slab by an outline inside its edge) and continue into the same zone on the next or previous layer; on the Hidden layer's and the tray's slabs h/l walk along and j/k go by rows (PREFS-107). Space lifts the chip under the cursor; j/k then move it along the zone and across layers, h/l to another zone; its place shows its name, faded, in an accent outline. Space or Enter drops it, Esc (or losing focus) puts it back. A widget dropped past groups drawn at its zone's end (a group with no anchor, `before: ""`) lands after them: those groups re-anchor on the widget (PreferencesModel.endGroupsPassed, GroupsModel.anchorOn); before, the drop meant "the zone's end", the widget's own place when it was the zone's last, and nothing moved. Shift with h/l (typed as capitals) or with Left/Right moves the chip under the cursor to the next zone at once, as a lift, that step and a drop would; Shift with j/k or with Down/Up moves it to the same zone on the next layer down or up at once (On the bar, On demand, Hidden; a tray icon on to In the tray and back), keeping its place in the zone as the inspector's Layer does; at the first or last layer nothing happens. On the Hidden slab Shift with h/l changes the widget's zone instead (PREFS-107). The cursor goes with the chip; on an empty tile Shift only moves the cursor. Enter pulls the chip forward to the inspector, and again pushes it back (PREFS-101); x sends it to the Hidden layer, and on the Hidden layer back to the layer it came from this session (else On the bar). Pointer: press a chip and move more than 6 px to lift it; a flat copy follows the pointer and the landing place opens as an outline before the first chip whose middle the pointer has not passed (in the tray's row: reading left to right, row by row); release drops it; a press elsewhere puts it back, and so does Esc with the button still down: the copy goes, the landing place closes, nothing is written, the cursor stays on the chip, and the release that follows does nothing (moving on with the button down lifts nothing either) until the next press. A click (press and release without moving) pulls the chip forward. The legend (PREFS-103) names the keys: "Along the layer, across the camera" ("Along the layer" without one), "Down the zone, on to the next layer", "Pick up, then drop", "shift h l Move it to the next zone", "shift j k Move it to the next layer", "enter Pull it forward to inspect" ("enter esc Push it back" while a chip is out), "Send it to the hidden layer" ("Bring it back from the hidden layer" on a hidden chip), "Next layer", with the slab's own h/l and j/k lines on a slab (PREFS-107); during a lift: "Along the zone, on to the next layer", "Zone" (on a slab "A row down or up, on to the next layer", "Along the slab"), "Drop", "Put it back". Code: PreferencesModel.stepLayer(), stepLayerLift(), nextLayer(), layerPos(), slabPos(); LayoutEditor moveCursor, homed, step, carryLayer, gapAt, keyPressed, cancel(), pressSpent.

- **PREFS-28** A drop on another layer writes the destination layer's `powerbarVisibility` on that entry and puts it at the drop position in the destination zone. Code: PreferencesModel.move()

- **PREFS-29** A drop in the same layer and zone reorders the entry and keeps every other setting (format, size, instance key). The Zone and Visibility choices of the tile change one thing at a time: a visibility change keeps the zone and place; a zone change appends to the zone's end.

- **PREFS-30** Hiding keeps the widget's settings. Showing it again returns it to the layer it was hidden from, remembered for the session; with none remembered, On the bar.

- **PREFS-31** x or Delete on the cursor's item hides it, or, if hidden, shows it again (PREFS-30).

- **PREFS-32** g on a group icon opens the Groups page with that group selected; on a widget it does nothing.

## Configuration persistence and healing

- **PREFS-79** Every Preferences change is applied at once to the bar's own copy of its config, and written to shell.json once drag, glide and slider settle have finished (Bar.writeConfig). A control writes the value it shows; only an undefined value deletes its key (`Preferences.setOption`).

- **PREFS-80** When the host refuses a write the bar returns to the saved config and the footer shows the message of PREFS-5.

- **PREFS-81** The layout is `bar.layout.<left|center|right>`: entries with `id`, `powerbarVisibility` (`shown`, `managed`, `hidden`; absent = shown), `powerbarInstance` (unique key) and the widget's own settings. Defaults for absent keys: BarModel.options() (autoHide false, autoHideActivity true, largeBar false, spacingMode slots, onDemand drawer, animations true, keyLegend true, dotsBadge true) and AppearanceModel.ranges. Configs Powerbar ran on before the "On the bar" default keep what they show: integration.md.

- **PREFS-82** The style is `bar.powerbarStyle` (`flat`, `floating`, `islands`); an old `powerbarIslands: true` reads as islands and is replaced on the next write.

- **PREFS-83** A group's member can name the widget property its badge counts and its urgent state (`powerbarBadgeProperty`, `powerbarUrgentProperty`); the Groups page's inspector edits them for the widget row under the cursor in the chosen group (PREFS-116; placeholders "Count from: detected" and "Urgent from: detected").

- **PREFS-84** The widget registry rescan after a bar switch is integration.md's.

## IPC functions for preferences

- **PREFS-85** `omarchy-shell powerbar preferences` opens Preferences on the focused monitor (the first screen if none matches); if open, it reopens them there, on the current page.

- **PREFS-86** `closePreferences` closes them; nothing happens when they are closed.

- **PREFS-87** `preferencesPage <n>` opens Preferences if closed and shows page n (1 Layout, 2 Groups, 3 Look, 4 Behavior, 5 Tuning while `powerbarTuning` is on); a number above the last page shows the last page (Behavior, or Tuning when it is there; the old General, Appearance and Bar pages are Look and Behavior now) and below 1 leaves the page as it is.

- **PREFS-88** `groupsPreferences` opens Preferences (if closed) and shows the Groups page, also when already open.

## Multi-monitor behavior

- **PREFS-89** There is one Preferences window. `preferences` and the Preferences button of a bar open it on the monitor they belong to; opening it elsewhere moves it.

- **PREFS-90** Changing the focused monitor does not move an open window; the next `preferences` opens it on the newly focused monitor.
