One bar page, tiled like your desktop

Powerbar spreads how the bar looks and behaves over two tabs, General and Appearance, as single-column forms that both scroll. The only bar preview is on Appearance, and it scrolls out of view as soon as you edit anything. The proposal merges both tabs into one Bar page: tiles laid out with your Hyprland gaps and corners, and your real bar pinned above them, so every change shows where you make it. The preview is drawn from your own widget captures on your wallpaper and theme.

The rule

Every size comes from a token you already set for your desktop, so the window looks like the rest of it and follows your theme when it changes.

Why one page. General and Appearance answer one question: what does my bar look like, and how does it behave. Measured offscreen at your window size, General needs 776 px in a 654 px view and Appearance needs 1062 px, so both scroll. As tiles, all 20 controls fit in one 1180 × 790 window with room to spare, so the extra tab only costs a switch.

Why the preview is pinned. Preferences scrolls the focused control into view. On Appearance that pushes the preview off the top before you touch the first control: 0 of 11 Appearance controls keep it in view. Pinned above the tiles, it shows every change, including a slider draft before the bar applies it.

Why your gaps and corners. A tile that sits 8 px from its neighbour with your window corners reads as part of your desktop, the way your tiled windows do. When you change gaps_in or rounding, the window follows; there is no number of its own to drift.

Why the tiles are these sizes. Size encodes importance: the preview spans the whole width, Style gets half a row because it changes the most pixels, and the other tiles take a third or a quarter. A setting that only matters when another is on (Show on activity, shadow softness) sits inside that setting's tile and dims, so nothing jumps when you switch it.

Edge cases

Each one rendered with the same code as the mock above, at a smaller scale. Today's behaviour is described in the text.

Motion

Preferences is opened a few times a week, but each control in it is used many times in a row, so tiles themselves never move.

  1. One switch.

    Everything follows powerbarAnimations (Animations, in the Behavior tile), read as bar.animationsEnabled.

  2. Motion never carries information alone.

    A dependent setting dims and its controls stop taking focus; the dimming is what tells you, not a slide.

  3. Off means idle.

    Nothing in the window runs per frame. The preview redraws only when a value or draft changes.

How it maps onto Powerbar

A sequence, in the order it can land. Each step leaves Preferences working.

  1. Add PrefsTile.qml.

    A BorderSurface with title, an optional header ToggleSwitch (switchChecked, switched()), an optional reset action shown while changed is true, and default property alias content. Fill Qt.alpha(Color.popups.text, 0.035), border 0.1, radius Style.cornerRadius, padding Style.spacing.rowPaddingX.

  2. Add PrefsBento.qml.

    A GridLayout of 12 columns (6 under 1100 px of window, 1 under 820) with columnSpacing and rowSpacing of 2 × gaps_in; Omarchy's Style.gapsOut is half of gaps_out, so read gaps_in the same way in Style.qml or fall back to Style.space(8). Tiles set Layout.columnSpan per column count and Layout.fillHeight so a row shares one height. Move PrefsForm's j/k walking and reveal logic here, and make Tab jump to the next tile's first control.

  3. Give PrefsSlider a compact mode.

    Label and value on one line, no description and less padding, for tiles. The draft, settle and key handling added in this pass stay as they are.

  4. Merge General and Appearance into one Bar page.

    In Preferences.qml, pages becomes Layout, Groups, Bar; preferencesPage maps 3 and 4 to Bar. BarPreview moves out of the scrolling area and sits above PrefsBento; it already draws slider drafts.

  5. Fill the tiles.

    Style: BarStylePicker. Position: a new screen picture with four edge buttons (one Tab stop, h/l walks edges) and the center-pin dropdown. Size: Large bar, Spacing, Space between widgets. Shape: Corner rounding, Borders as the thickness row's switch. Surface: Background opacity, Background blur, Readable over windows. Shadow: header switch, softness, strength. Behavior: Autohide with Show on activity nested, Animations. On demand: In a drawer or In the bar, Count on the dots.

  6. Replace the reset buttons with tile actions.

    Shape resets powerbarIslandBorders, powerbarIslandBorderWidth and powerbarIslandRounding; Surface and Shadow split today's AppearanceModel.reset into two key lists. Each action is shown only while one of its keys is set.

  7. Extend the offscreen audit.

    docs/rendering-bench, scenario sliders, already logs page heights and whether the preview is in view at each focus stop. Add the Bar page and assert: no scroll at 1180 × 790, preview in view at all 20 stops, Tab reaches each tile.

  8. Check on the desktop.

    Open with qs ipc -p /usr/share/omarchy/shell call powerbar preferencesPage 3, walk it with only the keyboard and then only the pointer, at your screen and with a theme that has rounding.

What the research says

The references this proposal borrows from, and the part each one contributed.

  • NN/g, Tabs, used rightFewer tabs are better; group panel content the way people think of it. Look and behaviour of the bar is one group.
  • GNOME HIG, Boxed listsGroup preferences by topic, not by frequency; one control per row, two at most.
  • UI Patterns, Live previewKeep the preview updating through the whole interaction; it invites safe exploration. Hence the pinned hero.
  • UX Tigers, Direct manipulationImmediate, incremental, reversible feedback. Drafts show in the preview at once and Esc undoes them.
  • SaaSFrame, Designing bento grids that workTwo or three tile sizes, the most important content in the largest tile, one consistent gutter.
  • Pocket-lint, macOS Control CenterSettings as tiles of a few sizes, each with its switch in place. The model for header switches on Shadow.
  • Material 3, SlidersDiscrete sliders show tick marks and an exact value; used for Border thickness's six steps.
  • Apple, Get to know the new design systemConcentric corners: controls inside a tile round at the tile radius minus its padding.
  • Hyprland wiki, Variablesgaps_in is the gap between windows (twice it between two windows), rounding their corners: the two tokens the grid uses.
